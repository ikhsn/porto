### cs.payments.type
FINTECH/PAYMENTS · SEED TO SERIES-C

### cs.payments.headline
Payments orchestration for 10+ global firms

### cs.payments.subtitle
Global payment teams needed faster launches, lower costs, and reliable routing across multiple processors.

### cs.payments.description
Led vendor sourcing and implementation of payment orchestration across cards, bank transfers, e-wallets, and disbursements. Designed phased routing with redundancy, compliance, and risk alignment.

### cs.payments.challenge
Global fintech and marketplace firms faced fragmented payment stacks across cards, bank transfers, e-wallets, and disbursements. Each new market required months of vendor negotiation and integration work, delaying revenue.

### cs.payments.approach
Led end-to-end vendor sourcing, contract negotiation, and phased implementation of payment orchestration layers. Designed routing systems with redundancy, compliance guardrails, and risk alignment across multiple processor relationships.

## CASE STUDY: PAYMENT OPS OPTIMIZATION

### cs.paymentops.type
MARKETPLACE & SAAS · SEED TO SERIES-B

### cs.paymentops.headline
Reduced chargeback loss and saved six figures annually

### cs.paymentops.subtitle
A growing marketplace was haemorrhaging revenue through high chargebacks, failed payments, and slow settlements.

### cs.paymentops.description
Led product and ops audit, optimization roadmap, and back-office automation redesign. Revised processor contracts, fraud/chargeback playbooks, tooling, and monitoring dashboards.

### cs.paymentops.challenge
Payment failure rates were above industry benchmarks, chargeback and fraud losses were climbing, and the ops team lacked real-time visibility. Processor contracts were unoptimised and settlement timelines were slow.

### cs.paymentops.approach
Conducted a full product and ops audit, then led an optimisation roadmap covering back-office automation, revised processor contracts, routing and retry logic improvements, fraud/chargeback playbooks, and monitoring dashboards.
